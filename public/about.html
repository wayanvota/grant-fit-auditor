Grant Fit Auditor

Use AI to apply to fewer grants, not more.

Most AI grant tools reward volume. They help teams draft faster, submit more, and create a larger pile of weak applications. Grant Fit Auditor makes a different argument as working software.

Humans decide what the organization will pursue or refuse. AI extracts requirements, checks fit against the profile it has been given, infers scoring pressure, and names the evidence gaps that would weaken a submission.

The point is not to automate judgment. The point is to protect judgment from being buried under deadline pressure and tool-driven volume.

What the tool does

The auditor accepts grant guidelines and a short NGO profile. It returns requirements, eligibility checks, inferred scoring criteria, ranked gaps, and one of three recommendations: pursue, refuse, or pursue with work.

What the tool refuses

The tool refuses to define organizational strategy, invent unstated funder expectations, or certify whether internal evidence is accurate. Those are human responsibilities.
