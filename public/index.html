Editorial argument

Built to refuse, not to apply faster.

Let people set strategy, then use AI to expose requirements and gaps before a team wastes weeks on a weak fit.

What this tool will not do

  • It will not score or rank your applicants. It gives no number and no order of merit.
  • It will not decide or recommend who to fund. A reviewer confirms every routing state.
  • It will not apply criteria you did not publish. It reads only the criteria you provide.
  • It will not tell you whether claims are true. Verification remains human work.

What AI won't do here

  • Define what your organization will pursue or refuse. That is a leadership choice about mission discipline and opportunity cost.
  • Surface missing requirements not stated in the RFP. Unwritten funder expectations require human judgment and relationship knowledge.
  • Audit your evidence file for accuracy. The tool can compare text. It cannot certify whether your evidence is true.
