Grant Fit Auditor

Use AI to apply to fewer grants, not more.

Built to refuse, not to apply faster.

Editorial argument

The grant-writing industry sells volume. This tool makes the opposite case: let people set strategy, then use AI to extract requirements, infer scoring pressure, and expose gaps before a team wastes weeks on a weak fit.

What AI WON'T do here
  • Define what your organization will pursue or refuse. That is a leadership choice about mission discipline and opportunity cost.
  • Surface missing requirements not stated in the RFP. Unwritten funder expectations require human judgment and relationship knowledge.
  • Audit your own evidence file for accuracy. The tool can compare text. It cannot certify whether your internal evidence is true.
