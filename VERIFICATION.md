## Fictional NGO Profile

Mama Mobile Health is a fictional composite designed for demonstration. It is constructed to be plausibly competitive on some criteria and visibly gapped on others.

The intended demo result is `PURSUE WITH WORK`, with visible gaps around Direct-to-Participant delivery, technology-facilitated GBV risk practices, privacy documentation, monitoring and evaluation at 50,000-user scale, and regional expansion beyond Kenya.

## Fictional Reviewer Worklist

Reviewer mode uses the fictional Riverbend Opportunity Fund 2026 Economic Mobility Open Call and five fictional US applicants:

- Lakeshore Workforce Collaborative and Cuyahoga Small Business Lab demonstrate `MEETS STATED CRITERIA` with `ROUTE TO FULL REVIEW`.
- Grand Prairie Skills Center demonstrates `ELIGIBILITY UNCERTAIN` with `NEEDS HUMAN CHECK` because legal status, operating budget, and operating history are missing.
- Sun Valley Opportunity Network demonstrates `OUTSIDE STATED SCOPE` with `CONFIRM AGAINST SCOPE` because its stated geography conflicts with the call.
- Great Lakes Arts Alliance demonstrates `OUTSIDE STATED SCOPE` with `CONFIRM AGAINST SCOPE` because its stated program conflicts with the call.

The saved worklist was generated by an actual `gpt-5-mini` reviewer batch. It renders immediately at `?reviewer-demo=1` and is clearly labeled as a fictional saved example. Running reviewer triage generates a new provider-backed worklist from the visible inputs.

The first provider run correctly treated missing IRS-standing evidence as uncertainty for Lakeshore and Cuyahoga. Their fictional profiles were tightened with explicit public-charity and IRS-standing claims, as directed by the work order's fixture-correction rule. A second provider run produced the expected 2 full-review, 1 human-check, and 2 scope-confirmation routes without changing the reviewer safeguards or schema.

## Editorial Framework

The tool audits against the editorial framework Wayan Vota described for the piece "Grant teams need to use AI to apply to fewer grants, not more."

The software boundary is deliberate:

- Humans define what the organization will pursue or refuse.
- AI extracts explicit requirements and scoring pressure from the RFP.
- AI flags gaps from the provided profile.
- Humans verify evidence, funder context, and strategy.
