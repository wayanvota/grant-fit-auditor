About

A decision tool, with visible limits.

The auditor turns supplied opportunity text and organization facts into a cited triage brief. It can decline on explicit disqualifiers, pause for material closeable gaps, pursue when the supplied evidence is clean, or require a human check when evidence cannot support a judgment.

Evidence before confidence

Hard stops retain exact quotes and source sections. Filing figures retain their tax year and source. Missing application volume stays missing.

No stored profiles

The application uses no database and does not retain submitted profiles, calls, or results.

People decide

The output supports staff triage. It does not certify eligibility, verify internal evidence, or replace leadership judgment.
