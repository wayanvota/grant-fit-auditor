Built to refuse, not to accelerate volume.

One tool, two directions

The same fit question, read from both sides.

Applicant teams decide whether an opportunity deserves their effort. Review teams route attention against the criteria they actually published.

I am applying

Pressure-test the opportunity before drafting.

Compare the call with your mission, programs, geography, evidence, partnerships, and known gaps.

  • Extract cited requirements and eligibility conditions.
  • Surface scoring pressure and competitiveness gaps.
  • Get a pursue, refuse, or pursue-with-work recommendation.

I am reviewing

Route reviewer attention without scoring applicants.

Compare a published call with a small applicant set, using categorical review states and two-sided citations.

  • See uncertain and outside-scope cases first.
  • Open every flag to its criteria and applicant sources.
  • Confirm or overturn each routing disposition as a human.

The refusal is the feature

What this tool will not do

Set organizational strategy

Leadership decides which opportunities fit the mission and opportunity cost.

Rank applicants by merit

Reviewer mode uses categorical routing states, never an order of worth.

Invent unpublished criteria

The audit can only apply the requirements present in the text it receives.

Certify that claims are true

Evidence verification and final judgment remain human responsibilities.

Stateless by design. No database stores criteria, profiles, or worklists.
