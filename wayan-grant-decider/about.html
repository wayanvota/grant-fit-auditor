About Grant Fit Auditor

Grant Fit Auditor is a working demonstration of a simple claim: grant teams should use AI to apply to fewer grants more strategically.

The tool extracts requirements, checks eligibility, infers scoring pressure, identifies gaps, and recommends whether the organization should pursue, refuse, or pursue with work.

The boundary

Humans set strategy. AI audits the text it is given. The tool refuses to define what an organization should pursue, invent unstated funder expectations, or certify whether internal evidence is accurate.
